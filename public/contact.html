Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about connecting with the author, obtaining signed editions, and staying part of the Dream Quest community.

All Products

How quickly do you respond to messages?

I aim to respond to all reader messages within 2–3 business days. During busy periods — such as around new releases or book events — it may take a little longer, but every message is read and valued.

Can I request a signed edition?

Yes! Signed copies are available on a limited basis. Reach out through the contact form with your request and preferred title. I'll confirm availability and walk you through the ordering process directly.

Are your books available on Kindle Direct Publishing?

My titles are available through multiple channels including Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and select retailers. You can purchase digital and print editions directly from this site or through your preferred platform.

Do you participate in book club discussions?

Absolutely — book club conversations are one of my favourite ways to connect with readers. I'm available for virtual Q&A sessions and group discussions. Send me a message with your club's details and preferred dates to get started.

How can I stay connected and hear about new releases?

The best way to stay in the loop is to join my reader list via the newsletter sign-up on this site. You'll be the first to hear about new titles, events, and exclusive content — no spam, just stories.

Can I reach out for media or interview requests?

Yes — I welcome media inquiries, podcast invitations, and interview requests. Please use the contact form and include relevant details about your publication or platform. I'll respond as promptly as possible.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale orders?

Bulk orders for schools, libraries, or community groups can be arranged. Get in touch through the contact form with your quantity and purpose, and I'll be happy to discuss options and pricing tailored to your needs.

Where can I leave a review or share feedback?

Reader reviews mean the world to independent authors. You're welcome to leave a review on Amazon, Barnes & Noble, or Goodreads — or simply drop me a personal note here. Honest feedback helps more readers discover the work.

Is there a way to follow along on social media?

Yes — you can find Dream Quest Media Books on social platforms where I share writing updates, reading recommendations, and behind-the-scenes glimpses of the creative process. Links are in the footer of every page.
